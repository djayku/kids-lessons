Plants are ALIVE! And they need special things to grow big and strong!

🌟 The 4 Magic Things Plants Need:

1. ☀️ SUNLIGHT — The Energy Source! The sun gives plants energy to make their own food! Without sun, plants become weak and pale! 🌻 Plants turn toward the sun to get as much light as possible!

2. 💧 WATER — The Drink! Plants need water to stay alive, just like us! Water travels through the stem to every part of the plant! 💧 Without water, plants droop and wilt (get all soft and sad)!

3. 🌍 SOIL / DIRT — The Food Bank! The soil holds nutrients (good stuff) that plants eat through their roots! 🌱 The roots dig deep into the soil to hold the plant steady!

4. 💨 AIR — The Breath! Plants breathe air too! They take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen (which WE breathe)! 🌬️ Good air flow helps plants stay healthy!

What happens if a plant DOESN'T get one of these things?

MissingWhat happens
☀️ SunlightPlant becomes weak, pale, can't make food
💧 WaterPlant droops, leaves get dry and crispy
🌍 SoilPlant has nowhere to put roots, falls over
💨 AirPlant can't breathe, gets sick
