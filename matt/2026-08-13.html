Democracy was invented in Athens, Greece! 🏛️

Key ideas from Ancient Greece:

  • 👥 "Demos" (people) + "Kratos" (rule) = Rule by the People
  • The Greeks invented the Olympics (every 4 years!)
  • Famous philosophers: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle — they invented Western philosophy
  • They built the Parthenon — a temple to the goddess Athena

What did the Greeks give the world?

  • 📐 Geometry (Euclid)
  • 💊 Medicine (Hippocrates — the Hippocratic Oath!)
  • 🏺 Theatre (comedies and tragedies)
  • 📖 Fables (Aesop's Fables)
