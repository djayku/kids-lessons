🌍 Why Does Our Planet Need Protecting?

Our Earth is amazing, but it's in danger from pollution and overuse of resources:

ProblemWhat it isWhy it matters
🏭 PollutionDirty smoke, chemicals, plastic in the oceanMakes air, water, and soil unhealthy
🐾 Loss of wildlifeAnimals and plants dying outEvery species plays a role in nature
🌡️ Climate changeEarth is slowly getting warmerCauses storms, floods, droughts
🗑️ Too much wastePeople throw away too much plastic and foodLandfills fill up, oceans get polluted
🌲 Cutting down treesForests are cleared for farms/citiesAnimals lose homes; less clean air

♻️ THE 3 R's — REDUCE, REUSE, RECYCLE!

These are the three most important things we can do!

1️⃣ REDUCE — Use Less! 📉

  • Turn off lights when you leave a room 💡
  • Don't leave taps running while brushing teeth 🚰
  • Use both sides of paper before recycling 📄
  • Say NO to plastic bags — bring your own bag 🛍️
  • Don't waste food — take only what you can eat 🍽️

2️⃣ REUSE — Use Again! 🔄

  • Use a reusable water bottle instead of buying plastic bottles 💧
  • Use old jars as storage containers 🫙
  • Donate old clothes and toys instead of throwing them away 👕
  • Use scrap paper for notes 📝
  • Repair things instead of replacing them 🔧

3️⃣ RECYCLE — Turn Old into New! ♻️

  • Paper → New paper 📄
  • Plastic bottles → New bottles, clothing, or playground equipment!
  • Glass bottles → New glass bottles 🫙
  • Metal cans → New metal products!
  • Food scraps → Compost → New soil → New plants! 🌱
