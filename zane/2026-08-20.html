🚢 THE AGE OF SAIL — 1400s–1700s

  • Big sailing ships explored the world!
  • Christopher Columbus crossed the Atlantic in 1492 in a sailing ship called the Santa María 🛶
  • Ships could travel about 150–200 km per day with good wind.

🚂 THE INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION — 1800s

This changed EVERYTHING!

YearInventionWhy it's amazing
1804Steam locomotive 🚂 (by Richard Trevithick)First train — moved along rails!
1825First public railway 🛤️People could travel faster than ever before
1830Steamships 🚢Ships that didn't need wind — could travel in any direction!
1886First car 🚗 (Karl Benz, Germany)A carriage without horses!
1903First aeroplane ✈️ (Wright Brothers)Humans FLEW!
