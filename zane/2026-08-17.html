🎯 What is a Fraction?

A fraction shows us one or more parts of something that has been divided into equal parts.

    1         ← Numerator (how many parts we have)
  ──────
    4         ← Denominator (total equal parts)
PartWhat it means
Numerator (top number)How many equal parts we are talking about
Denominator (bottom number)The total number of equal parts the whole is divided into

🍕 Pizza Fractions — The Tastiest Way to Learn!

Imagine a pizza cut into 4 equal slices. Each slice is 1/4 (one quarter) of the pizza.

  🍕 Fractions with a Pizza (4 equal slices)

  Whole pizza          3/4 of the pizza       1/2 of the pizza
  (4/4)                (3 quarters)           (a half)
  ┌─────────┐          ┌─────────┐            ┌─────────┐
  │ ╱ ╲    │          │ ╱╲▓▓   │            │ ╱ ╲    │
  │╱  ╲   │          │╱ ▓▓   │            │▓▓╱ ╲   │
  │──────│          │──────│            │──────│
  │╲  ╱  │          │ ╲  ╱  │            │  ╲  ╱ │
  │ ╲╱   │          │  ╲╱   │            │   ╲╱  │
  └─────────┘          └─────────┘            └─────────┘
  All 4 slices         3 of 4 slices          2 of 4 slices
  = 4/4 (whole)       = 3/4                   = 2/4 = 1/2

🎂 Common Fractions — Know These!

FractionWordsWhat it means
1/2One half1 out of 2 equal parts — a whole divided in 2
1/3One third1 out of 3 equal parts
2/3Two thirds2 out of 3 equal parts
1/4One quarter1 out of 4 equal parts
2/4Two quartersSame as 1/2!
3/4Three quarters3 out of 4 equal parts
1/1One wholeAll parts — the complete thing!

🔄 Equivalent Fractions — Different Names for the Same Amount!

Look at this — these fractions are actually the SAME amount!

  1/2 = 2/4

  Whole          Divided in 2        Divided in 4
  ┌───┐          ┌───┐                ┌───┐
  │░░░│          │░░░│  → 1 half      │▓▓│▓▓│  → 2 quarters
  │░░░│   =      │   │                │  │  │  = same amount!
  │   │          └───┘                ├──┼──┤
  └───┘                                └───┘
  1/2           = 2/4

Other equivalent fractions to remember:

  • 2/2 = 1 whole (the same!)
  • 2/4 = 1/2
  • 3/3 = 1 whole
