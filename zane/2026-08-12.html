Your body is one of the most incredible machines ever built — and you live inside it! 🦸

There are trillions (millions of millions!) of tiny living things called cells working together inside you. They are so small you can't see them without a microscope!

🫀 THE HEART — Your Body's Pump

  • Your heart is about the size of your fist — and it never stops beating!
  • It beats approximately 70-100 times per minute — that's over 100,000 beats every day!
  • It pumps blood all around your body through veins and arteries (tubes).
  • Blood carries oxygen (from the lungs) and food energy to every part of your body.

🫁 THE LUNGS — Your Body's Air Filters

  • You have two lungs — they fill up with air when you breathe in.
  • The lungs take oxygen from the air and pass it into your blood.
  • When you breathe out, your lungs push out carbon dioxide (CO₂) — the waste gas.
  • Your lungs have millions of tiny air pockets called alveoli — if you spread them out flat, they'd cover a whole tennis court!
ActionWhat happens
Breathe IN (inhale)Lungs fill with air, oxygen enters blood
Breathe OUT (exhale)Lungs push out CO₂ (the waste gas)

🧠 THE BRAIN — Your Body's Command Centre

  • The brain is the boss of your whole body!
  • It controls everything: thinking, memory, movement, emotions, breathing, heartbeat, and even your dreams when you sleep!
  • Your brain has three main parts:
PartWhat it does
🧠 CerebrumThinking, learning, memory, imagination, 5 senses
🔄 CerebellumBalance, coordination, moving smoothly
🌊 Brain stemAutomatic things: heartbeat, breathing, digestion
  • Your brain is soft like jelly — that's why you have a skull (hard bone) around it to protect it! 🛡️

🫃 THE STOMACH — Your Food Processor

  • When you eat, food goes down a tube called the oesophagus (food pipe) into your stomach.
  • Your stomach is like a mixing machine — it churns food around with acids and enzymes (special chemicals) until it becomes a soft mush.
  • This takes 2–4 hours!
  • Then the food moves into the small intestine, where the nutrients (good stuff!) are absorbed into the blood.
  • The waste goes to the large intestine and then leaves the body!
